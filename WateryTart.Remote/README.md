## Proposed Parts List
(Links to follow eventually)
* Wemos Lolin D32 (MCU)
* ssd1306 i2c (Display)
* 0.1 uF capacitor
* SW-200D tilt switch
* 12 6x6x6 push buttons
* lipo battery

Mech parts
* 3x M2.5? M3? heat set inserts & screws

Everything will be hand solderable
